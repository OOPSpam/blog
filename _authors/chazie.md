Chazie Baniquid is a Technical Content Marketer at oopspam, where she has written most of the guides on this blog.

Her guides cover stopping spam in WordPress form plugins such as WPForms, Gravity Forms, Elementor Forms and Fluent Forms, blocking VPN, data center and country-based submissions, fixing Cloudflare Turnstile and CAPTCHA problems, and keeping bots out of email lists in Mailchimp, beehiiv and Brevo. She also covers fake orders and card testing in WooCommerce, SureCart and FluentCart.

Each guide walks through the actual settings, step by step with screenshots, so site owners can set up protection themselves. Chazie enjoys exploring new ideas and turning technical topics into approachable, practical content.
