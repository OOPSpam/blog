Daniella Attinella is the co-founder of oopspam and the designer behind its brand.

She joined Onar Alili as co-founder as oopspam grew from an open-source project into a product, and created the oopspam brand you see today. She leads its visual design, so everything oopspam puts out looks and feels like oopspam.

Daniella also runs her own design studio, [Ella Ella](https://ella-ella.studio/). On this blog she writes practical guides on blocking spam and unwanted submissions in WordPress forms. Read more about [the people behind oopspam](https://www.oopspam.com/about#daniella).
